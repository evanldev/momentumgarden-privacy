MomentumGarden

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

MomentumGarden is a habit-tracking app published by EvanLApps. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter into the app.

In short: MomentumGarden does not collect, transmit, or share your information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information the app stores

When you use MomentumGarden, the app saves the following on your device so that it can show you your progress:

  • The name and intention you enter during setup
  • The habits you create, including their names, descriptions, and colours
  • Which habits you have marked complete, and on which dates
  • Any reflections you write
  • Your app settings, such as your chosen theme

This information is stored in your device's local app storage. It is not sent to us, and it is not sent to any third party.

Information we do not collect

MomentumGarden has no account system, no login, and no server. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage analytics. There are no advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking tools in the app.

Network access

The app does not require an internet connection to function and does not make network requests to send or retrieve your personal information.

Exporting your data

The app includes an option to export your data as a file. When you use it, the file is created on your own device and saved wherever you choose. We never receive a copy. Once exported, that file is yours to manage, and anything you do with it afterwards is outside the app's control.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app has stored at any time using the reset option in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its stored data from your device. Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no request you need to send us.

Children's privacy

MomentumGarden is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no information at all, no personal data from any user, of any age, reaches us.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be described here rather than made silently.
